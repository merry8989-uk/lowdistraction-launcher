Appearance: icons, your own text, and 60+ themes

- New Appearance screen in Settings.
- App list style: name only (as before), icon only (shown as a grid), or icon
  and name together.
- Icons: size, shape (original, circle, rounded, square) and outline thickness.
- Names: text size, bold, and letter spacing - make them as big or as bold as
  you like.
- 60+ built-in themes, dark and light, no downloads needed.
- Theme creator: pick your own background, text, secondary text and accent
  colours to build a theme of your own.
- Optional drifting glow, now coloured by the theme.
