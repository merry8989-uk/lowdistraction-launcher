The log now uses the unified diagnostics schema, nested and OpenTelemetry-shaped

- Every record is nested the way the plan specifies: source, severity, category,
  context, correlation, error, metrics and privacy, instead of one flat object.
  Schema version 2.0.0 — a breaking change.
- severity is uppercase, as the plan writes it: TRACE, DEBUG, INFO, WARN, ERROR,
  FATAL.
- Each record now carries an event_id and an observed_timestamp, and a readable
  message, so a log viewer has a line to show rather than a bag of fields.
- source groups the app id, platform, environment, version, build, OS, device and
  network; context carries the screen, a coarse feature name and the operation.
- Where a property goes is decided by its name in one table, and CI checks that
  table against the schema on every build, so the two cannot drift apart.
- Caught exceptions are now recorded. The plan names captureException and it was
  the real gap: uncaught crashes were logged, but every failure the app handled
  and swallowed went unmentioned. Two places report it to begin with — a hidden
  app that would not open, and the torch shortcut failing.
- A report now waits for the background writer to catch up before it is built.
  Without that, the events of the last few milliseconds were the ones most likely
  to be missing from it.
- Nothing was invented to fill the plan's fields: request_id and trace_id stay
  null because there are no requests and no tracing, and contains_personal_data
  records that redaction ran rather than proving anything. The storage stays a
  local file, and the reasons are written down in docs/logging.md.
