Quiet over payment & government apps, and files in search

- The bubble now disappears on its own over payment and government apps, and
  the bubble's gestures pause while one of them is open. Tap-to-pay apps are
  detected automatically; you can add more from Settings.
- New optional strict mode: also switch the accessibility service off over
  those apps (off by default, since Android needs you to switch it back on).
- The right search bar now also looks inside files and folders you share with
  the app - documents, media and sub-folders, matched by name.
