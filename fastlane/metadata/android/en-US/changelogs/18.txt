Sensitive apps: full list, auto-detection, and a one-tap toggle

- The built-in list now covers UPI and wallet apps, banks, NBFCs and lenders,
  brokers, insurers, and central and state government services.
- New: apps are also matched by name and by tap-to-pay support, so anything
  installed that looks like a payment or government app is covered even if it
  was never listed.
- New: long-press any app on the home screen and pick "Hide the bubble over
  this app" or "Show the bubble over this app" to force it either way.
