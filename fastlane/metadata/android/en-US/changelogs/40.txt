The log now has an explorer — filter it, search it, count it

- Diagnostics gains a Log explorer: filter by severity and by category, and
  search across everything an event says — its name, category, severity, screen,
  session, and every property, both the key and the value, so "package" finds the
  property and "com.whatsapp" finds the app.
- A line above the list always says how much is showing and how much is being
  left out, so a screenshot cannot be mistaken for the whole log.
- The whole log's shape is printed as counts: every severity and every category,
  including the ones sitting at zero. "No errors" is an answer.
- Tap any event to see everything it recorded, grouped the way it is stored:
  context, correlation, error, metrics and attributes.
- Severity is coloured from your theme, so errors read as errors in both light
  and dark themes without breaking either.
- All the deciding — matching, counting, summarising — lives in one small file
  free of Android, and 21 new unit tests cover it. The screen only draws what
  that decides.
- Nothing new is collected and nothing is uploaded. The explorer reads the log
  that is already on the phone, which is the only kind of dashboard this app can
  honestly have.
