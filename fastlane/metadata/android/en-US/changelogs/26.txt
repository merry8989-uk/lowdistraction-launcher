Hidden apps can be opened again

- Settings > Hidden apps: tapping a hidden app now asks what you want to do with
  it - open it, or put it back in the list. Before, tapping it only unhid it and
  said "Ring updated", which was wrong on both counts.
- Opening a hidden app leaves it hidden, so it stays out of the home list.
- The pinned grid and pinned-apps screens now say "Saved" instead of borrowing
  the bubble ring's message.
