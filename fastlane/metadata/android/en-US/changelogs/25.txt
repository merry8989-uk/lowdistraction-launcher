Custom pinned grid, and sorting the app list

- The pinned grid is now yours to shape: choose 1 to 10 rows and 1 to 4 columns
  from Settings > Pinned apps > Grid size. A fresh install stays at 3 rows by 2
  columns, and nothing is ever pinned on its own - the grid stays empty until
  you pin something yourself.
- New sort control above the search box: name A-Z, name Z-A, recently installed,
  recently updated, most opened, recently used, and most time used.
  Everything except the last one works with no permission at all. "Most time
  used" reads the system's usage figures, so it needs Usage access, which you
  grant by hand; until then that option simply stays inactive.
