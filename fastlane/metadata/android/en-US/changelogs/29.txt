The pause before opening: a tick, and your own timer length

- A soft tick now sounds once a second while the timer runs. The last three
  ticks are a little brighter, so you can hear it running out.
- Settings gains a Tick sound switch, so the ticking can be turned off on its
  own, without turning the timer off.
- Settings gains Timer length. Set the breather anywhere from 1 second to 2
  minutes — however long you want it. It was fixed at 7 seconds before.
- The pause itself can still be switched off entirely, as before.
