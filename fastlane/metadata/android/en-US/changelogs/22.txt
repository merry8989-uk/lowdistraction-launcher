Wheel sounds and pinned apps

- The assistive wheel's chime is now a choice of 16 sounds: Soft chime, Bell,
  Marimba, Glass, Wood block, Pluck, Drop, Ping, Halo, Bubble, Kalimba,
  Vibraphone, Ceramic, Tine, Ripple and Night. Pick one from Settings and hear
  it right away.
- Pin up to four apps. They get their own row at the top of the home screen,
  drawn bold and half again as large as everything else.
- Long-press any app and choose "Pin to top", or manage the list from Settings.
