Low Distraction is a minimal, text-only Android home screen.

Instead of a grid of icons you get a quiet list of app names, the time and
date, and a search box. Type to filter; when only one app matches it opens by
itself. Long-press an app for App info, Uninstall or Hide.

There is also an optional assistive bubble: a draggable floating button that
opens a spin-wheel ring of your favourite apps, three nested circles for
Back / Home / Lock, and a long-press panel of device shortcuts (torch, sound,
brightness, Do Not Disturb, tea mode and more).

Privacy: the app declares no INTERNET permission at all, so it cannot make a
network request — no analytics, no crash reporting, no trackers. Its only
dependencies are AndroidX.

Features:
* Text-only, distraction-free app list
* Search that opens the app automatically when the query narrows to one match
* Clock and date header
* Hidden apps
* Optional assistive-touch bubble with a spinnable app ring
* Gestures: swipe for dialer / camera
* Long-press anywhere on the home screen to open Settings

Low Distraction is free software, released under the AGPL-3.0 licence. No
ads, no accounts, no tracking.
